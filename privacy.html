DRAFTA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

DRAFTA does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking.

  • The app downloads a public, read-only list of football players from our backend (Supabase) to power gameplay. These requests contain no personal information.
  • Your in-game progress (record, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our servers.
